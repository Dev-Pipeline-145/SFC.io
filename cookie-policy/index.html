Last Updated: October 7, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how DevPipeline (“we,” “us,” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on salesforceconsultants.io. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help a site remember preferences or support measurement and advertising. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels.

How We Use Cookies

When you land on the Site, we may run:

  • Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-JXKDK1RBS0) to understand how the Site is used.
  • Standard commercial advertising pixels for measurement and ads, when configured.
  • Essential cookies needed for security, load balancing, or basic site delivery.

We display a privacy notice on your first visit. You can opt out at any time with Do Not Sell or Share My Info in the notice or the footer. We also honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as an opt-out. The inactive property ID G-8ZNLKDLFEC is not used.

Essential Cookies

Our hosting infrastructure may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies required for security, load balancing, or basic site delivery. Your privacy choice is stored in local storage on your device (sfc_privacy_choice).

Analytics and Advertising Cookies

Unless you opt out, Google may set cookies such as _ga and related Google Analytics identifiers. Advertising pixels, when used, may set cookies or read device identifiers for ads and measurement. Use Do Not Sell or Share My Info to turn these off.

Managing Cookies and Opting Out

Use the privacy notice, or choose Do Not Sell or Share My Info in the footer. You can also control cookies through your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Blocking all cookies may affect how some websites function.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat that as an opt-out of analytics and advertising cookies. We do not change Site behavior based on older “Do Not Track” signals beyond the choice you make here.

Updates

If we add cookies or similar technologies, we will update this policy. The privacy notice and opt-out remain available.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Cookie Policy

DevPipeline | SalesforceConsultants.io
Last Updated: October 1, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how DevPipeline (“we,” “us,” or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on salesforceconsultants.io. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They can help a site remember preferences or support basic functionality. Similar technologies include local storage, session storage, and pixels.

How We Use Cookies

We limit cookies to what is needed to run the Site and, if you agree, to measure usage:

  • Analytics cookies only with consent: Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-JXKDK1RBS0) loads only after you accept analytics in the cookie banner. Until then, analytics storage is denied and Google Analytics is not loaded.
  • No advertising cookies: We do not use advertising networks or retargeting pixels on this Site.
  • No third-party embed cookies: We do not embed YouTube, Google Maps, Apple Maps, or reCAPTCHA, which could set cookies from those providers.
  • Icons: Navigation and social icons are inline SVG served from this Site, not Font Awesome CDN.

Essential Cookies

Our hosting infrastructure may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies required for security, load balancing, or basic site delivery. These are typically session-based and do not track you across other websites. Your analytics choice is stored in local storage on your device (sfc_cookie_consent) so we remember Accept or Reject.

Analytics Cookies We May Use After Consent

If you accept analytics, Google may set cookies such as _ga and related Google Analytics identifiers. These help us understand which pages are used. They are not used for ads on this Site. The inactive property ID G-8ZNLKDLFEC is not used.

Third-Party Cookies We Do Not Use

As of the date above, this website does not intentionally set or permit:

  • Advertising or retargeting cookies
  • Font Awesome CDN tracking
  • YouTube or video platform cookies
  • Google reCAPTCHA cookies
  • Map provider cookies (Google Maps, Apple Maps)

Some pages may still request web fonts from Google Fonts. That can involve a connection to Google even when analytics is rejected. We are moving toward system fonts to reduce that.

Managing Cookies

Use the cookie banner, or choose Cookie Settings in the footer, to accept or reject analytics. You can also control cookies through your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Blocking all cookies may affect how some websites function.

Do Not Track

We do not use behavioral advertising or cross-site tracking. We do not change Site behavior based on “Do Not Track” browser signals beyond the consent choice you make here.

Updates

If we add cookies or similar technologies in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, obtain consent before placing non-essential cookies.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

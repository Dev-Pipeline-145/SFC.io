Privacy Policy

DevPipeline | SalesforceConsultants.io
Effective Date: July 24, 2026 | Last Updated: October 1, 2026

1. Overview

DevPipeline (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates salesforceconsultants.io (the “Site”), a DevPipeline division. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect on the Site, why we collect it, and how you can control it. It should be read together with our Cookie Policy.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context advertising. We do not use advertising networks, retargeting pixels, session replay, keystroke logging, or chat tools that intercept your communications.

2. Information You Provide Directly

If you request a $500 discovery or otherwise use a contact form, you may provide:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and organization
  • Organization type, Salesforce usage, needs, timeline, and message

We use this information only to respond to your request, schedule the discovery, and provide related Salesforce consulting information. Submitting a form is optional. We do not require an account.

If you contact us outside the Site (for example by email or phone), we use the information you send only to respond to you.

3. Analytics (Only With Consent)

If you accept analytics cookies, we load Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-JXKDK1RBS0) so we can understand aggregate site usage, such as pages visited and approximate location derived by Google. Analytics cookies are not set until you click “Accept analytics.” If you reject, we do not load Google Analytics.

Google may process this data as a service provider. We configure IP anonymization and do not use Google Analytics for advertising personalization on this Site. You can change your choice using Cookie settings in the footer.

4. No Tracking, Monitoring, or Interception of Communications

In connection with the California Invasion of Privacy Act (California Penal Code §§ 630–638), we confirm that the Site does not:

  • Record, intercept, or eavesdrop on communications except for information you choose to submit in a form or email;
  • Use session replay, keystroke logging, mouse-tracking, or chatbot technology that captures visitor interactions;
  • Deploy advertising pixels or SDKs that share visitor data with advertising networks; or
  • Permit any third party to monitor Site communications except Google Analytics after you consent, as described above.

Analytics is inactive until you accept. That consent mechanism is the banner described in this Policy and the Cookie Policy.

5. Server Logs and Hosting

Our hosting provider may automatically and temporarily record limited technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and access times) in standard server logs for security and operational purposes. These logs are not used to build marketing profiles and are not sold.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Essential cookies or similar technologies may be used by hosting infrastructure for security, load balancing, or basic delivery. Analytics cookies are used only with consent. Details are in the Cookie Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (including California), you may have the right to request access, deletion, or correction of personal information we hold about you, or to opt out of sale/sharing (we do not sell or share for advertising). To exercise these rights, email info@devpipeline.com.

9. Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change, we will revise this Policy, update the “Last Updated” date, and, where required by law, provide additional notice or obtain consent before the change takes effect.

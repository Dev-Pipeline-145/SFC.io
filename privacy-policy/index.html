Privacy Policy

DevPipeline | SalesforceConsultants.io
Effective Date: July 24, 2026 | Last Updated: October 7, 2026

1. Overview

DevPipeline (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates salesforceconsultants.io (the “Site”), a DevPipeline division headquartered in Orem, Utah. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect on the Site, why we collect it, and how you can control it. It should be read together with our Cookie Policy.

We display a privacy notice when you visit. Google Analytics and commercial advertising pixels may run when you land. You can opt out of sale/sharing and these technologies at any time.

2. Information You Provide Directly

If you request a $500 discovery or otherwise use a contact form, you may provide:

  • Name, email address, phone number, and organization
  • Organization type, Salesforce usage, needs, timeline, and message

We use this information only to respond to your request, schedule the discovery, and provide related Salesforce consulting information. Submitting a form is optional. We do not require an account.

If you contact us outside the Site (for example by email or phone), we use the information you send only to respond to you.

3. Analytics and Advertising (Opt-Out)

When you visit, we may load Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-JXKDK1RBS0) and standard commercial advertising pixels so we can understand site usage and measure or deliver ads. These technologies can collect device identifiers, approximate location, pages viewed, and similar usage data.

You can opt out with Do Not Sell or Share My Info in the privacy notice or the footer. We also honor Global Privacy Control (GPC). After you opt out, we deny analytics and advertising storage and do not load those tags on later visits from that browser unless you allow them again.

4. Communications We Do Not Intercept

The Site does not:

  • Record, intercept, or eavesdrop on communications except for information you choose to submit in a form or email;
  • Use session replay, keystroke logging, mouse-tracking, or chatbot technology that captures visitor interactions; or
  • Use generative AI to interact with you on this Site.

5. Server Logs and Hosting

Our hosting provider may automatically and temporarily record limited technical information (such as IP address, browser type, and access times) in standard server logs for security and operational purposes. These logs are not sold.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Essential cookies may be used by hosting infrastructure for security, load balancing, or basic delivery. Analytics and advertising cookies may run on landing unless you opt out. Details are in the Cookie Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live in the United States, including under the Utah Consumer Privacy Act, you may have the right to request access, deletion, or correction of personal information we hold about you, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and targeted advertising.

To opt out of sale/sharing and advertising/analytics cookies, use Do Not Sell or Share My Info. To exercise other rights, email marketing@devpipeline.com.

9. Generative AI

This Site does not use a generative-AI chatbot to interact with visitors. We do not currently publish images on the Site that we know to be substantially produced by generative AI. If we add such images later, we will label them in line with applicable Utah law.

10. Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change, we will revise this Policy, update the “Last Updated” date, and, where required by law, provide additional notice.
